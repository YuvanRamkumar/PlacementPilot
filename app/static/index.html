How scoring works

The job description is split into requirements. Each one is checked against your resume and marked:

Met
Every core part is backed by a line in your resume.
Partial
Some parts, or a closely related skill, are backed.
Missing
Nothing in your resume backs it.

Your match is must-haves met, plus half a point for each partial, out of all must-haves. Every quote shown is checked against your resume, so nothing is invented. Your email, phone and links are removed before anything is sent to the AI.
