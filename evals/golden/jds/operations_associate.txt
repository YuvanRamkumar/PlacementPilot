Operations Associate

We need a detail-oriented Operations Associate to keep our business running smoothly.

Must-have:
- Proficiency with Excel and Google Sheets for reporting and data entry
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Process documentation experience
- Attention to detail and organisational skills

Nice-to-have:
- Basic SQL knowledge
- Project management tools (Jira, Asana, or similar)
- Data analysis or business intelligence experience

Responsibilities:
- Maintain operational dashboards and weekly reports
- Coordinate with cross-functional teams on project delivery
- Document processes and create standard operating procedures
